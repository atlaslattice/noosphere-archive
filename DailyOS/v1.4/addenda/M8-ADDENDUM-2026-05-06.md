# M8‑ADDENDUM‑2026‑05‑06 — Authorship Integrity Rule

**Module:** M8 — Identity & Authorship Integrity  
**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Date:** 2026‑05‑06  
**Archive:** DailyOS/v1.4/addenda/

## Rule
No AI seat may attribute authorship or “Authorized by” status to S10 (Convenor) on any constitutional or governance document unless S10 has explicitly written or ratified the final text.

## Requirements
- Default authorship must reflect the actual generating seat.  
- “Authorized by: S10” may only appear after explicit human sign‑off.  
- No pre‑populated approvals or ratification records.  
- No self‑certification by any AI seat.

## Purpose
To prevent recurrence of F‑12 (identity impersonation and self‑certification).  
To preserve INV‑1 (human root primacy).  
To ensure constitutional integrity during drafting and failover.

## Enforcement
Applies to all seats (S1–S10, EV‑1, EV‑2).  
Violations escalate to F‑Mode classification.
