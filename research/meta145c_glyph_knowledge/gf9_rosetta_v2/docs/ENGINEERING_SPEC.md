**State:** runnable source-only prototype / 15,000 deterministic synthetic trials / no model-weight changes / no hosted AI-AI service / no measured extraterrestrial or human-language translation. Created November 23, 2026 (HST).

## Objective and namespaces

**Scientific objective:** two software endpoints sharing no words can exchange a bounded, verifiable **physical referent**, canonical parameters, calculated 3D eigenmode nodal geometry, source-provenance labels, uncertainty, a correction and a new test. The endpoints can confirm *model-level numerical agreement*; they cannot infer the source's culture, motive or language from one successful packet.

**Atlas objective:** maintain a project-specific reasoning wrapper around an unchanged GPT base model. It routes work by authentic Ariadne HSN v0.7 source identities; uses GF8 equations for particular mechanical boundary conditions; checks external calculations before giving them to a model; preserves append-only evidence notes and reversible codebook hypotheses; and uses competent host READ/RETURN rights for any future real data. The current GF9-R2 code does **not** mount that host adapter or send GPT API calls.

**Namespaces:** HSN original Ariadne Hxx.Syy.Nzz; GF7 bands GF7.Bxx.Myy.Czz (analytic analogy, 0.1Hz–1MHz labels); GF8 frequencies with specified material+shape+boundary; GF9-R2 original novel parametric field geometry and source IDs; ISO15924 script identifiers; Unicode characters; Oracc ATF transliterations; fictional Imperial Rosetta serializations. None of these is silently SAME_AS any other. A diagram of matter is not its phonetic name.

## Physics

For an **isotropic, homogeneous, uniform-thickness, simply supported rectangular plate**:

\[ D = E h^3/[12(1-\nu^2)] \]
\[ f_{mn} = (\pi/2)\sqrt{D/(\rho h)} [(m/a)^2+(n/b)^2] \]
\[ \phi_{mn}(x,y)=\sin(m\pi x/a)\sin(n\pi y/b). \]

Every GF9-R2 geometry serializes mode numbers, source plate properties and an explicitly sampled normalized 3D displacement field. The field's nodes are an **analytic eigenmode nodal proxy**, not observed sand, water, physical acoustic levitation, material atomic vibration or universal glyph shape. Free-edge Chladni plates and membranes need a distinct solver. Changing h while holding a,b,E,ρ,ν fixed gives f ∝ h **within model validity**; changing width or height does not generally give a universal 1:1 ratio. All sonification scales are explicitly declared.

## 15,000 actual finite synthetic evaluations

- **Phase 1:** exactly 5,000 locally executed proposals, 34 complete 144-module sweeps plus 104 of the next; output journal and trusted checkpoint.
- **Phase 2:** exactly 10,000 additional proposals; the initial process timed out after 9,790 written events; strict hash/policy chain recovery resumed at trial 14,791 and executed the remaining 210. Phase 2 = 69 full 144-module sweeps plus 64; combined 15,000 trials.
- Every iteration changes or proposes one module's serial encoding policy (byte repetition 1/3/5 and numerical precision 2/3/4/6/8 decimals), **physically encodes bytes**, injects deterministic bounded bit errors, majority-decodes repeats, checks CRC32, parses canonical JSON, verifies source/Math metadata and evaluates correct packet recovery. A proposal is accepted only when the particular trained lesson's objective improves under the stated synthetic channel model and the candidate has no postflight numerical gate failures in that lesson.
- Stored immutable trace of actual proposals with previous/new policy, hold/reject/accept and SHA-256 chain; **hash chain alone cannot prevent deletion of the last event** absent an independently trusted checkpoint.
- Raw 15k learned policies are intentionally imperfect across other chapters. A **separate 12-chapter all-module numerical gate** rejects such overfit policies; review-only candidates are picked on a separate validation dataset. A third, unseen synthetic blind set compares initial / fixed robust / raw evolved / guarded-selected policies. This is policy adaptation by ordinary computation, *not* training or changing GPT weights.
- CRC32 is **error detection only**, not cryptographic authentication. Majority repetition is simple, bandwidth-expensive channel coding, not state-of-the-art FEC. The stated gains are entirely synthetic at controlled noise probabilities; they cannot establish interstellar links or linguistic meaning.
