## Universal Operating Boundary

All Modules 21–27 are Governance Instruments. They are proposed frameworks for voluntary coordination, standards, certification, audit, advisory simulation, and internal governance discipline.

No Module 21–27 network or body may command governments, regulate sovereigns, enforce law, operate markets, control supply chains, control infrastructure, provide clinical care, issue emergency orders, conduct diplomacy, compel data sharing, override host-jurisdiction law, or claim authority over non-participants.
