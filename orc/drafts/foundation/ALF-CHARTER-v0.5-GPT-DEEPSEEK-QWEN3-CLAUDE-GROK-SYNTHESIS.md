## Core Synthesis Line

The Foundation is offered as a gift to the world, not a proprietary control layer. It may steward open standards, research, audit, simulation, interoperability, certification, and public-good coordination methods, but it does not command governments, regulate sovereigns, operate infrastructure, conduct diplomacy, or claim authority over non-participants.
