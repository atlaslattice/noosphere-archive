## Executive Verdict

Atlas Lattice Foundation Charter v0.2 is strong as a mission and public-governance scaffold, but it is not ready for ratification. It should be treated as a Foundation formation draft, not constitutional canon.

Primary concern: the draft mixes foundation governance, Convenor authority, constitutional invariants, multi-Convenor future design, non-militarization, and legal entity structure in one document before the legal container and D-127/oversight issues are stabilized.

## Major Risks

### R1 — “Founder Discomfort Clause as Constitutional Invariant” may create registry drift

The clause is important, but calling it a constitutional invariant may conflict with the existing invariant registry unless formally assigned an INV number through the proper process.

**Patch:** Call it a Charter hard-stop clause for now. Promote to invariant only through the formal invariant pipeline.

### R2 — D-127 is tabled but Charter recreates oversight architecture

The Global Stewardship Council and future multi-Convenor system start to recreate the same governance surface as D-127 while D-127 is explicitly tabled.

**Patch:** Mark all council/board/oversight architecture as legal-formation scaffolding, not active constitutional authority.

### R3 — Multi-Convenor future is under-specified

The draft says future Convenors can run projects through the methodology, but does not define scope, authority, conflict rules, revocation, jurisdiction, or relationship to the Founder.

**Patch:** Move multi-Convenor mechanics to a separate later doctrine or bylaws appendix.

### R4 — “No single entity controls the Foundation” conflicts with Founder veto until handoff

Both ideas can coexist, but not without staging language.

**Patch:** Separate pre-handoff Founder-supervised phase from post-handoff open-governance phase.

### R5 — Non-militarization is too broad for existing defense-adjacent work

The project has Golden Dome / defense / hypersonic / infrastructure resilience language elsewhere. A blanket “no offensive capabilities” clause is safer than broad “non-militarization,” but still needs definition.

**Patch:** Use “no offensive or unlawful military capability development; defensive resilience, humanitarian protection, and critical infrastructure security may be permitted under separate review.”

### R6 — Legal foundation structure cannot be finalized without counsel

The draft references Linux Foundation, Microsoft, neutral institution legal review. Good. But until counsel reviews entity structure, board roles, fiduciary duties, IP, tax status, and international activity, this is not an operating charter.

**Patch:** Add “legal non-operative draft” language.

### R7 — “Founder / Architect / Meta-Convenor” terminology needs cleaning

The draft uses Founder, Convenor, meta-Convenor, architect. These terms need hierarchy.

**Patch:** Define:
- Founder = David Sheldon
- Convenor = constitutional/canon authority within Atlas Lattice
- Meta-Convenor = optional descriptive term, not legal office unless later defined
- Executive Director/COO = operational role, no canon authority
