## S10 Clarification

S10 clarified that the best-case operating model does not require S10 to personally run day-to-day execution. Instead, S10’s practical responsibilities would remain similar to the current role:

- define the methodology;
- review and refine architecture;
- convene the Council;

- a COO / operating lead;
- legal counsel;
- finance / accounting support;
- domain experts;
- technical implementation teams;
- certified conveners;
- node teams;
- project managers;
- audit and compliance support;
- execution pipelines.

## Governance Meaning

The best-case Founder role is not CEO-by-default and not operational bottleneck.

The intended mature posture is:

> S10 remains Founder / Convenor / methodology steward, while properly formed human teams execute operations through clear pipelines.

## Execution Pipeline Model

Execution pipelines may include:

1. Intake — project, node, partner, or learner request.
2. Triage — determine T1 / T2 / T3 status.
3. Methodology application — certified convener or team applies Atlas method.
4. Expert review — domain experts validate claims.
5. Council review — advisory multi-seat review where useful.
6. Legal / safety / compliance review — counsel or compliance lane as applicable.
7. Implementation planning — COO / PM / node team executes.
8. Audit trail — GoldenTrace / F-ledger / versioning / evidence vault.
9. Feedback loop — lessons learned improve curriculum and methodology.

## Core Line

> S10 stewards the methodology and constitutional root. A COO, experts, conveners, and pipelines execute the work.
