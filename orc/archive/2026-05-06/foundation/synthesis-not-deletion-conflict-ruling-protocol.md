## S10 Clarification

S10 clarified that the correct workflow is synthesis, not deletion.

No prior draft, module, clause, seat contribution, or review should be silently discarded merely because a newer synthesis exists. Conflicting material should be preserved, compared, and surfaced for S10 ruling.

## Process Rule

S6 shall:

1. Preserve source drafts as provenance artifacts.
2. Synthesize compatible language into the next draft.
3. Flag conflicts explicitly.
4. Ask S10 for a ruling where a conflict changes authority, legal posture, founder role, governance structure, licensing, sovereign jurisdiction, ratification state, or operational authority.
5. Avoid deleting, suppressing, or overwriting conflicting language without S10 direction.
6. Maintain Zero Erasure and provenance throughout.

## Conflict Handling

When conflicts appear, classify them as:

- **Wording conflict:** same meaning, different language — S6 may propose merged wording.
- **Structural conflict:** different governance architecture — S6 must surface options.
- **Authority conflict:** changes who can decide or ratify — S10 ruling required.
- **Legal conflict:** may affect entity law, fiduciary duties, licensing, compliance, or jurisdiction — counsel review required.
- **Canon conflict:** changes invariant/doctrine registry, ratification state, or D-series status — S10 ruling required.

## Core Line

> Do not delete. Synthesize. If there is a conflict, ask S10.

## Caveat

This note records S10’s process clarification. It does not ratify or amend any Charter draft by itself.
