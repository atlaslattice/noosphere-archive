## Required S6 Corrections Before Integration

### 1. Ratification posture too hot

The draft says `Ready for Legal Review & Founder Ratification`, `Founder signature`, and `Public deployment + Track II policy circulation`. This must be downgraded to:

> Founder Review Draft / Legal Non-Operative / Ratification Record Blank / Not Public-Deployment Ready.

### 2. Founder role permanence needs sharper wording

The draft says Founder may delegate constitutional and operational authority. This needs the v0.6 patch:

> The Founder / Convenor role is permanent. Delegation is operational, not ontological.

### 3. D-126 should not be revived as operative

The draft references D-126 succession instruments. Current S10 posture is D-126/D-127 tabled/replacement intent. Any D-126-style continuity instrument must be described generically as a future pre-ratified voluntary continuity instrument unless S10 explicitly revives D-126.

### 4. Founder Discomfort Clause should not be called invariant yet

Use Founder Principled Review Right / Pre-Delegation Hard Stop. Do not label as constitutional invariant unless registry-ratified.

### 5. Sovereign dialect language needs host-law safety

Qwen’s `no local compliance requirement may override` language should be patched to certification/participation language:

> Where host law conflicts with ALF conformance requirements, the dialect cannot be certified or must suspend/modify participation. ALF does not instruct parties to violate local law.

### 6. Hardware sovereignty / self-destructing keys needs legal/security review

Preserve as a candidate trust-layer design, not a blanket global requirement.

### 7. GSC/TSC arbitration must respect fiduciary law

Post-delegation governance must be counsel-approved and subject to entity law, fiduciary duties, bylaws, and applicable jurisdiction.
