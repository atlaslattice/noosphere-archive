## S6 Correction / Boundary

Grok’s proposed sentence — “All governance bodies created by this Charter are subordinate to the Pantheon Council and the human Convenor until voluntary delegation” — is directionally right but should be patched for legal safety.

Preferred v0.6 wording:

> Before voluntary delegation, all governance bodies proposed or created under this Charter are advisory and subordinate to the Founder / Convenor for constitutional, architectural, ratification, and activation decisions. The Pantheon Council is advisory unless separately granted defined authority. After legal formation or voluntary delegation, all bodies operate only within counsel-approved bylaws, fiduciary duties, entity documents, and ratified governance instruments.

This preserves Grok’s root-protection goal without accidentally making the Pantheon Council legally superior to a future board or fiduciary governance body.
