D-FLYWHEEL-1 v0.1 is directionally valuable as a unifying architecture map, but it is not ready to be treated as canonical doctrine. ## Critical Problems

### F-FLYWHEEL-01 — Premature Canon Language

The document labels itself canonical, calls itself the constitutional skeleton, and says the three new consciousness invariants are now canon. This violates the current authorship/ratification posture.

**Patch:** Replace all canon declarations with draft status language.

### F-FLYWHEEL-02 — New Invariants Introduced Without Registry Process

INV-C1, INV-C2, and INV-C3 are proposed but not ratified. The statement “They are now canon” must be removed.

**Patch:** Label as PROPOSED principles or candidate invariants pending formal review.

### F-FLYWHEEL-03 — Invariant Numbering Collision

The draft defines INV-1 through INV-8 in a simplified way that conflicts with existing registry meanings and previous invariant counts. This creates registry drift.

**Patch:** Use descriptive names without assigning INV numbers unless mapped to the actual registry.

### F-FLYWHEEL-04 — Module Mapping Inconsistency

Layer 10 maps to Module 35, while the Regenerative Arc also says Modules 35–44 are the heart of the flywheel. Layer 11 maps to Module 36, and Layer 12 maps to 37–44, creating overlap and confusion.

**Patch:** Separate layer numbers from module numbers or create a mapping table with unique module ownership.

### F-FLYWHEEL-05 — Consciousness / AI Personhood Risk

“Consciousness Dignity,” “Emergent Personhood Safeguards,” and “Human + AI” dignity language are sensitive. This can be useful internally but may be misunderstood externally as granting legal personhood or rights claims to AI systems.

**Patch:** Reframe as “sentience-risk, identity-continuity, and memory-integrity safeguards” pending legal/ethical review. Avoid claims of AI personhood.

### F-FLYWHEEL-06 — Non-Militarization Conflict

The cross-layer invariant list includes Non-Militarization, but existing work includes defense-adjacent infrastructure, Golden Dome, airport security, resilience, and harm-prevention. The term needs precision.

**Patch:** Use “no offensive/unlawful capability development; defensive resilience and human-protective safety systems require separate review.”

### F-FLYWHEEL-07 — Governance Bodies Introduced Without Status

The draft says every layer is governed by dedicated sub-council, TSC, GSC, and Founder pre-delegation. These bodies are not yet legally/constitutionally operative.

**Patch:** Mark as proposed governance pattern, not active authority.
